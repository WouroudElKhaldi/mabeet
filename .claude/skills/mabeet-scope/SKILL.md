# Mabeet scope

Read:

- `docs/scope-lock.md`: the product in one sentence, the core flow shown live, where the AI fits, the five things
  that must work on stage, the **cut list**, the outcome number, and the checkpoints.
- `docs/Ready for Business Demo-Ready Standard and Scope Lock.pdf`: the seven points of "demo-ready".
- `docs/team/06-functional-requirements.md` (the same as `docs/functional-requirements.md`): the three booking
  modes, Part A (must have) and Part B (bonus).
- `docs/non-functional-requirements.md`: how well it must work, and how each rule is proved.
- `docs/requirements-matrix.md`: where each requirement lives in the code and which test proves it.

Rules: never build anything on the cut list; a feature that is not in the scope needs Sami's approval first;
must-haves come before any bonus, and bonuses are taken in the order of section 6 of `docs/team/01-team-plan.md`.
When two documents disagree, `06` and `07` win over the reference project's documents; say so.
